Live rail playbook — 20 full pages

Twenty complete #/live page compositions — same rail, twenty game feels. Each demo below is a whole page: head chrome plus a rail of tickets in various states. What ships today is the ticket + flash base (02). Everything is Tower-token-only, motion-safe, simulated with honest shapes. Aesthetic: industrial-utilitarian with a playful whisper; pills for states, chips for facts. Chain, traced: main.go spanWriter+Hub → GET /v1/events (events.go) → pageLive() (console/static/app.js) → tower-ticket* (tower.css).

My recommendation: example 01 — the full regrouped waterfall rail. It folds in flash, stages, shimmer, error flags, pop, presence, heartbeat and odometer — everything from our regroup that shares one surface — while parking race, streak, sparkline, constellation and recap for Overview. Tell me to proceed and I build 01.
