Why it exists, and the constraints that shaped it

In one sentence

The goal is a finished, demoable, own-code project for a CV — built solo, on one RTX 4060 with 8 GB of VRAM.

Why it exists

Every design choice in the repo answers one of three constraints:

  • Solo time. Every slice is ≤ 3–5 days. Things that don't help the six MVP checkboxes are labelled Later and not touched.
  • 8 GB VRAM. Only one chat model fits in GPU memory at a time. So the router picks between a ~2 GB 3B model and a ~4.5 GB 7B-4bit model, and the swap rule is written down in docs/VRAM.md.
  • Independence. No wrapping a competing whole product (LiteLLM, TensorZero, DeepEval). Small primitives (a Postgres driver) are fine; a library that does the thing the CV story is about is not.

How it works in this project

The plan file docs/PLAN.md labels every section MVP or Later. The MVP is exactly six checkboxes:

  • Router between two local models with a rule you wrote.
  • Prometheus metrics + one Grafana dashboard.
  • MCP server with at least list_models and get_stats.
  • A small RAG app + golden set + hand-written faithfulness scorer.
  • A durable tracker that survives kill -9.
  • One trace view: pick a request, see every step.

The VRAM rule in plain words: one chat model resident at a time; the 137M embedding model is always resident because it's tiny.

Try it yourself

Open docs/VRAM.md and docs/PLAN.md §1. Count the six checkboxes. Everything else in the repo exists to close one of them.

Interview questions

Q Why local models instead of an API?
A Cost is $0, privacy is total, and the 8 GB VRAM limit is a real engineering constraint that forces a real routing decision — that's the point of the router.
Q What did you deliberately not build?
A Kubernetes, a vector-DB bake-off, a full eval framework, a production runbook. They're listed as 'Later' with a reason each, so the scope stayed shippable.
Q What does 'independence' mean in your plan?
A Don't import a library that does the CV-story thing for you. pgx (a Postgres driver) is fine; LiteLLM (a whole router) is not.

Remember

  • Constraints: solo, 8 GB VRAM, own code.
  • Six MVP checkboxes; everything else is Later.
  • One chat model in VRAM at a time.
