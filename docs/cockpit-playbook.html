Cockpit playbook — 20 full pages with history

Twenty complete #/cockpit compositions with conversation history built in — every past conversation stored, any of them continuable. Same cockpit every time (sticky send + live rail), twenty different histories. Gamified and simple. Aesthetic: industrial-utilitarian with a playful whisper; DFII 14. Anchor: the conversation list with the active thread pinned amber. Game lens: collection + continuation — your threads are a shelf you return to, and picking one back up is the core loop (open → continue → watch it land). Storage, decided: your own Postgres (conversations + messages, 90-day retention, user delete, PRIVACY updated) — no vendor. No Modus, no DataHub, no PostHog, no Atlan: wrong shape, data leaves the box, new bills.

My recommendation: example 01 — sidebar history cockpit. Conversation list left, sticky send+answer middle, live rail right (stacking on small screens). The shelf metaphor operators already know, one new surface, everything else reuses shipped pieces. Tell me to proceed and I build 01 (tables + sidebar + continue + delete).
